## Risk Management

### Technical Risks

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|---------|------------|
| Performance Degradation | Medium | High | Profiling, lazy loading, worker distribution |
| Browser Compatibility | Low | Medium | Feature detection, graceful degradation |
| Memory Constraints | Medium | Medium | Streaming, LRU caching, worker limits |
| Complexity | High | High | Modular architecture, clear interfaces |

### Scientific Risks

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|---------|------------|
| Overfitting | Medium | High | Cross-validation, regularization |
| Bias Introduction | Medium | High | Fairness metrics, diverse testing |
| Unpredictable Behavior | Low | Critical | Formal verification, extensive testing |
| Privacy Concerns | Low | Critical | Local-first, differential privacy |
